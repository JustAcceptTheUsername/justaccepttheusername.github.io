Project

Dungeon Conveyor

In developmentUnity 6 (6000.3), C#Team of twoRoguelike tower defense

You defend a Core on a hex-tiled planet by placing miners, conveyor belts and towers while waves of rock creatures attack. Every 10th wave brings a boss, and after each cleared wave you pick one of three upgrade cards.

View source on GitHub

Gameplay

  • Build miners, belts and towers on a rotating hex planet
  • Rock critters and ore golems hatch from meteors, chew belts and slam your Core
  • Boss waves with armour plates that break off as it takes damage
  • Upgrade cards after each wave: fire, lightning, frost and poison effects, or stat boosts
  • Maps grow as you clear stages

Technical highlights

  • Wave and boss system within a stage-based roguelike run
  • Card system with per-tower and global modifiers
  • Code-driven animation rig for rigid-part models, with hit flash, cracks and crumble-on-death
  • Runtime element palettes for recolouring enemies
  • Custom HUD with 9-sliced art and tweened feedback
  • An automated PlaytestBot for balance testing

Team

I focus on the code: most of the gameplay systems and architecture. My teammate Alex makes the art and 3D models, with occasional code contributions made with Claude Code.

Some assets, including a number of models, are AI-generated placeholders that will be replaced as the art develops. Parts of the code and documentation were also written with AI assistance and reviewed by the team.
