Privacy Policy

BlockBloom · Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game BlockBloom (the “App”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The App is developed by Deyan Staykov, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). If you have questions, contact me at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

1. Summary

  • I do not ask for your name, email address, or account details. The App has no login.
  • The App shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting provided by third parties. These services collect device information and identifiers as described below.
  • Where required by law (e.g. in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS, you are asked for permission before your device’s advertising identifier is used for tracking.
  • You can change your choices at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Options.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (best score, number of games played, sound/music/haptics settings, daily challenge progress and whether you purchased “Remove Ads”) are stored only on your device. I do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent for personalised advertising and for storing/accessing information on your device for advertising purposes. Consent is collected through a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) when you first open the App.
  • Legitimate interests for analytics, crash reporting and showing non-personalised ads, which keep the App free, stable and enjoyable.
  • Performance of a contract for processing in-app purchases.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Change consent: open Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • Advertising identifier: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset or delete it. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads: the optional “Remove Ads” purchase stops interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads you choose to watch remain available.
  • Access, deletion and objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because I do not hold identifying data myself, requests about advertising and analytics data are best directed to the providers listed above. You can also contact me and I will help.
  • California residents: I do not sell personal information for money. Sharing advertising identifiers with ad partners may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out using the controls above.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact me and it will be addressed.

7. Data retention and security

Third-party providers keep data according to their own retention policies (for example, Firebase Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months by default). Data is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. International transfers

The providers above may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. They use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
