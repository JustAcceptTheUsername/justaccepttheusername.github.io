Dungeon Conveyor

Roguelike tower defense · Unity 6 · C# · in development with an artist

You defend a Core on a hex-tiled planet by placing miners, conveyor belts and towers while waves of rock creatures attack. Every 10th wave brings a boss, and after each cleared wave you pick one of three upgrade cards. I focus on the code; my teammate Alex makes the art and models. Some assets are AI-generated placeholders for now.
